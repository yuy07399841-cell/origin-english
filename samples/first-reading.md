# The Habit of Noticing

Reading becomes more useful when attention is **sustained** long enough for a pattern to emerge. A subtle change in tone can reveal how a writer feels, even when the writer never states that feeling directly.

Context gives a word its local shape. Instead of collecting an isolated translation, a reader can ask what the word is doing in this particular sentence. That approach keeps meaning connected to experience.

Practice does not need to be dramatic. A quiet page, one honest question, and a small notebook can be enough for a beginning.
